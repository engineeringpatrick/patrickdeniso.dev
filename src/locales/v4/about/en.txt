here's my midnight thoughts dump of an about me section.

i was born in the town of [varese, italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varese), on september 28th, 2002, at around 2am (or that's what my mom told me, and i choose to believe her)

as a kid, i kept changing my answer to that classic question: “what do you want to do when you grow up?” usually, watching a movie would make me change my answer to whatever the protagonist was doing. i once wanted to be a boxer like rocky, and once a vampire after my older sister made me watch twilight when it came out.
but around middle school, i started tinkering with game development and eventually settled on becoming a programmer. i thought computers were cool.

i went to a [technical high school in italy](https://www.isiskeynes.edu.it/) (here's my [high school thesis lol](https://patrickdeniso.com/hs)), and i did really well in computer science classes. i guess it was my calling.
after graduating high school, i moved to montreal for [university](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concordia_University). i wanted a breath of fresh air, as i was tired of living in the same country. after that, my life became a whirlwind of new experiences. i interned in a bunch of cities, travelled to a bunch of places, and made a bunch of friends. i’ve always had this urge to get out of whatever feels familiar and see what else is out there. explore the unknown kind of thing. it sounds more dramatic than it is, but 8 year old patrick definitely wouldn’t have expected to leave his village in northern italy and end up here.

was i running away from something or just trying to broaden my horizons?

in my second year of university, i turned down the chance to intern in tokyo for three months as a [software engineer](https://www.diverta.co.jp/en/) because of a girl. a lot of that decision came from anxiety. it’s strange to think about that now, because that same anxiety has shaped more of my decisions than i’d like to admit. i tend to live at the extremes; everything is either black or white. maybe that's why, afterward, i started saying yes to almost every opportunity to leave that arose. that, in itself, brought more regret to my life.
i’m still trying to figure out what exists in the gray. i know there are colors in between, but somehow the extremes still feel more comfortable.

so i am now on this quest to find stability in life. the whirlwinds brought me to san francisco. i like this city, but i nonetheless still flee whenever i get some time off work. traveling is just so much more fun. and there goes my stability lol

as I haven't found that balance yet, i like having conversations that manage to be both slow and fast. the ones with plenty of comfortable silence and lots of adhd-filled ramblings.
if you've read thus far, reach out to me and let's get coffee! i actually don't drink coffee, but yk what i mean.

see ya!

- patrick
