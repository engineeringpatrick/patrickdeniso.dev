here's my midnight thoughts dump of an about me section.

i was born in the town of [varese, italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varese), on september 28th, 2002, at around 2am — or that's what my mom told me, and i choose to believe her.

as a kid, i kept changing my answer to that classic question: “what do you want to do when you grow up?” usually, watching a movie would make me change my answer to whatever the protagonist was doing. i once wanted to be a boxer like rocky, and once a vampire after my older sister made me watch twilight when it came out. but around middle school, i started tinkering with game development and eventually settled on becoming a programmer. i thought computers were cool.

i went to a [technical high school in italy](https://www.isiskeynes.edu.it/) (here's my [high school thesis lol](https://patrickdeniso.com/hs)), and i did really well in computer science classes. i guess it was my calling.

after graduating high school, i moved to montreal for [university](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concordia_University). i wanted a breath of fresh air, as i was tired of living in the same country. after that, my life became a whirlwind of new experiences. i interned in a bunch of cities, travelled to a bunch of places, and made a bunch of friends. i've always had this innate desire to escape, to get out of my familiar bubble and explore the unknown. it sounds more dramatic than it is, but eight-year-old patrick never would've thought he'd leave his small little village in northern italy and come all this way.

all this running away.

in my second year of university, i chose to stay in montreal for a girl instead of interning in tokyo for three months as a [software engineer](https://www.diverta.co.jp/en/). it would've been my first internship experience. i regretted it so much that i decided to run away at every new opportunity i was given. i took this to an extreme, and it eventually caused me to make another decision that filled me with even more regret, so now i resent that instinct a little. yeah, i have a cursed tendency to see things in black-and-white.

i am now trying to find balance in life, having never really had any. well, i still like money, and for now that brought me to san francisco. i like this city, but i nonetheless still flee whenever i get some time off work. travelling is just so much fun. there goes my balance.

i like having nice, slow conversations with people. the ones with plenty of comfortable silence and lots of adhd-filled ramblings. if anyone is reading this: reach out to me and let's get coffee! i actually don't drink coffee, but yk what i mean.

see ya!

- patrick
